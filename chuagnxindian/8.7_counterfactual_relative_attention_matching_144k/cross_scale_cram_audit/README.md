# Cross-scale CRAM comparative audit

Read-only audit of the completed 8.7 experiments.  No training, optimizer
step, checkpoint update, tau sweep, or data-split change was performed here.

Compared runs are the completed `10k` Mean / Hard-default and completed
`144k` Mean / Hard-default / Hard-adjusted pipelines, each followed by the
unchanged vanilla Stage-2.  The original 1.3G result at the matching scale is
the baseline.

`raw_maps/` contains newly extracted maps only for previously missing frozen
checkpoints. Existing Mean, baseline, and default-Hard caches are reused where
they were formally validated. `performance/`, `mechanism/`,
`residual_group/`, and `hardness_signal/` retain every sample-level source
used for the summary.

The four figures are generated by `figures/gen_fig_cross_scale.py` and are
exported as both vector PDF and 300-DPI PNG.
